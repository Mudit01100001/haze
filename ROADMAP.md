# Haze Roadmap

## Upcoming Features (Next Version)

### 1. True Chromatic Aberration (Lens Effect)
- Implement a true optical chromatic aberration (CA) effect that applies to the *edges* of the entire screen, simulating the color fringing of a physical camera lens.
- The effect will split RGB channels and shift them radially (primarily green and cyan channels), with maximum intensity at the edges of the screen and zero effect in the center.
- This will be a standalone feature independent of film grain, designed to enhance the physical, tactile feel of the blurred overlay.

### 2. Bootup Sound Design
- Add a subtle, premium bootup sound when Haze initializes or when the overlay is toggled.
- The sound will provide tactile auditory feedback to complement the visual focus shift.

## Ideas & Backlog
- Advanced multi-monitor support enhancements.
- Custom animation curves for the fade-in/fade-out transitions.
- Additional shader-based lens effects (e.g., subtle lens distortion/barrel distortion).
